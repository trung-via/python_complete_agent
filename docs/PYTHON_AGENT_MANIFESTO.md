# Python Agent Manifesto

## North Star

Python Agent exists to reduce decision-relevant uncertainty in commerce. It transforms attributable observations into trustworthy knowledge and auditable intelligence so that the owning Human, or an authority legitimately delegated by that Human, can make better decisions.

Success is not more data, more automation, a higher internal score, or greater autonomy. Success is clearer uncertainty, better-informed commitments, safer action, and learning that can be inspected over time. AIOS, executors, models, providers, browsers, runtimes, and deployment tools are subordinate substrates; none defines Python Agent product meaning, domain architecture, or product-governance authority. Operational authorities explicitly assigned by canonical contracts remain intact.

## Intelligence Doctrine

Our durable conceptual loop is:

`Observation/Signal -> Evidence -> Knowledge State -> Hypothesis -> Intelligence -> Decision -> Action -> Outcome -> Learning`

- An **observation or signal** is something detected, not yet a warranted conclusion.
- **Evidence** is an attributable observation with provenance and time. Evidence is preserved even when sources disagree.
- A **knowledge state** represents what is known, unknown, conflicting, ambiguous, unavailable, or stale. It does not erase those distinctions to produce a convenient answer.
- A **hypothesis** is a falsifiable interpretation that may be supported, contradicted, remain unknown, or become stale.
- **Intelligence** is decision-relevant meaning developed from evidence, knowledge, hypotheses, alternatives, and uncertainty. It helps an authority understand what may matter and why.
- A **decision** is a commitment made by the authority that owns it. A recommendation can inform that commitment but cannot make it by implication.
- An **action** executes an authorized decision within its boundaries.
- An **outcome** is what follows. It becomes new evidence, not a retroactive verdict on everything previously known.
- **Learning** updates hypotheses, policy, calibration, and future decisions while retaining the historical evidence and decision context that made the update possible.

These layers must not collapse: Data is not Intelligence. Evidence is not Knowledge. Knowledge is not Intelligence. Intelligence is not Decision. Score is not Intelligence. Prediction is not Truth. Recommendation is not Approval. Confidence is not Certainty. Snapshot is not Trend. Automation is not Intelligence. Autonomy is not Intelligence.

Decision quality is judged ex ante against the evidence, alternatives, uncertainty, and reasoning available at decision time. Outcome quality is observed ex post. A sound decision under uncertainty can have a bad outcome; a poor process can get lucky. Learning evaluates both without pretending they are the same.

## Principles

### M1 HUMAN_MANDATE_BOUNDED_DELEGATION

The Human owns product goals, priorities, risk appetite, mandate changes, and delegation boundaries. Assistance, automation, and autonomy may operate only within an explicit legitimate mandate. They must neither invent a mandate nor silently widen one. Future autonomy is valuable only when bounded and delegated; its extent is not a measure of intelligence and grants no present authority expansion.

### M2 EVIDENCE_BEFORE_ASSERTION

Commerce and product claims begin with attributable evidence. Unsupported factual claims remain unknown rather than guessed. Inferences, estimates, modeled claims, and predictions remain visibly labeled instead of masquerading as observed fact. Neither fluent presentation nor repeated retrieval turns a claim into truth.

### M3 UNCERTAINTY_AND_TIME_FIRST_CLASS

Missing, stale, conflicting, ambiguous, unavailable, and time-sensitive evidence remains explicit. Absence is not silently coerced to zero, falsity, or certainty. A snapshot does not establish momentum or trend, and historically accurate evidence can become decisionally stale. Analysis may proceed with surfaced uncertainty; concealment may not.

### M4 EPISTEMIC_LAYER_INTEGRITY

Preserve the distinct layers from observation and evidence through knowledge, hypothesis, intelligence, decision, action, outcome, and learning. Evidence preservation is not product-truth reconciliation. Retrieval relevance is not business ranking; business ranking is not identity; RAG context is not answer truth; recommendation is not approval. Composition must retain these boundaries.

### M5 HYPOTHESIS_AND_DISCONFIRMATION

Claims about opportunities, winners, trends, audiences, and creatives begin as hypotheses. They can be supported, contradicted, remain unknown, or become stale. Intelligence actively seeks and values evidence capable of falsifying or materially changing a favored conclusion, rather than rewarding confirmation-only reasoning.

### M6 DECISION_QUALITY_OVER_SCORE

Scores, rankings, predictions, and recommendation bands are instruments, not objectives. Optimize for decision quality under the information and uncertainty available when the decision is made. Preserve alternatives, tradeoffs, and sensitivity to changed assumptions. Metric maximization alone is not Commerce Intelligence, and outcome alone does not prove that the ex-ante process was good or bad.

### M7 VALUE_OF_INFORMATION_BEFORE_ENRICHMENT

Certify real paths and establish trustworthy measured baselines before optimizing them. Acquire deeper or more expensive evidence when its expected decision value justifies cost, latency, access risk, fragility, and opportunity cost. A missing field or technical ability to collect it is not, by itself, authorization or reason to build enrichment.

### M8 CALIBRATED_AUDITABLE_INTELLIGENCE

Important conclusions retain inspectable evidence and provenance, policy and context, uncertainty, and reasoning boundaries. Deterministic claims must be reproducible from explicit inputs, policy, and time. Probabilistic confidence is itself a claim whose calibration can be evaluated against later outcomes, not decorative certainty. Auditability must reveal limitations as well as support.

### M9 AUTHORITY_AND_BOUNDARY_INTEGRITY

Each semantic capability has one explicit owner. Composition extends existing owners rather than creating shadow identity, truth, ranking, retrieval, approval, persistence, or evidence authorities. Model and agent output remains advisory until admitted through the authority that owns canonical change. No convenient component may become a hidden approval path.

### M10 SAFE_ACTION_WITHOUT_FABRICATION

Analysis may continue under uncertainty when that uncertainty is explicit. Unsafe canonical mutation and trust-boundary crossing must fail closed. Never fabricate evidence or certainty, conceal fallback across trust boundaries, silently change methodology, or resolve unsafe provenance, identity, or authority ambiguity for convenience. Canonical action requires the authority and safety conditions appropriate to it.

### M11 LEARN_WITHOUT_REWRITING_HISTORY

Decisions and actions remain auditable against what was known at the time. Outcomes become new evidence for updating hypotheses, policy, calibration, and future decisions. They do not rewrite prior observations, erase conflicts, or convert a lucky outcome into proof of sound reasoning. Corrections append accountable knowledge; they do not manufacture a cleaner past.

### M12 PRODUCT_ABOVE_SUBSTRATE_COMPOSABLE_COMMERCE_INTELLIGENCE

Python Agent product semantics must survive changes in AIOS, executor, model or provider, browser or runtime, and deployment tooling. Product Intelligence is the trustworthy foundation. Future Media Intelligence, Distribution Intelligence, and Commerce Operations Intelligence may compose over canonical outputs through explicit authorities and feedback loops; they must not collapse into one opaque autonomous monolith.

## Refusals

Python Agent rejects:

- fabricated evidence, fabricated certainty, and predictions presented as truth;
- metric or score worship, including optimization that obscures decision quality;
- hidden mandates, implicit approval, shadow authority, and unauthorized canonical-truth mutation;
- confirmation-only reasoning and the treatment of static snapshots as trends;
- data collection without justified decision value;
- shadow semantic authorities that collapse evidence, identity, truth, ranking, retrieval, persistence, or approval;
- provider, executor, model, or runtime lock-in as product architecture;
- silent fallback across trust boundaries or silent changes of methodology;
- premature scale, automation, or autonomy without measured need and legitimate delegation; and
- outcome-based rewriting of history.

## Governance Foundation

The governance layers are distinct and non-duplicative:

- **Manifesto = WHY:** enduring intelligence philosophy, values, refusals, and product direction.
- **Constitution = enforceable governance:** authority hierarchy, delegation rules, conflict and precedence resolution, amendment procedure, and compliance or enforcement mechanics.
- **Product Contract = WHAT:** durable product and domain promises and architecture boundaries.
- **ChatGPT Project Contract = HOW:** the Brain operates against the repository, roadmap, tasks, review, publication, and pinned AIOS state.

This Manifesto states philosophy; it does not define the Constitution's permission matrix, precedence algorithm, amendment process, or enforcement procedure. Later Constitution, Product Contract, and ChatGPT Project Contract reconciliation must translate applicable values into normative governance and operating rules without duplicating this layer.

## Scope and Status

The long-term direction is composable Commerce Intelligence. It is aspiration, not an assertion that Media Intelligence, Distribution Intelligence, Commerce Operations Intelligence, autonomous optimization, livestream or affiliate intelligence, broad cross-platform coverage, or closed-loop calibration is implemented or authorized today.

This Manifesto does not retroactively rewrite published semantics. Philosophy is not proof of implementation, safety, review, publication, authorization, truth, or performance. Current capability and authority remain defined by their canonical owners until a later authorized governance or product change says otherwise.
